# ledger-signer

Signing certificates in custody, and every signature made with them, in a process of their own (spec §3.7,
ADR 0010; plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-v2-m2b-signer.md`).

- Owns schema `signer`, isolated in the signer's role (`[data] isolated_in = "signer"`): its roles belong to the
  `ledger_signer` login alone, whose tables (`certificates`, `custody`) no other app's login can read; the
  worker's fan-out moves its outbox and inbox. Id prefix `crt`; permissions `signer.certificates.read` and
  `signer.certificates.manage`.
- Contract: [`signer.proto`](../../contracts/proto/ledger/signer/v1/signer.proto) (`ledger.signer.v1.SignerService`),
  its code generated by `ledger-devtools sync` under `adapters/grpc/_generated/v1/` (never edited).
- Public surface: `SignerApi` ([`api.py`](src/ledger_signer/api.py)) for modules; fiscal-kit's `SignerPort` for
  channels; events `signer.certificate.installed`, `.retired` and `.expiring` (public `certificate.expiring`);
  HTTP under `/v1/certificates`, mounted by `api`.
- Settings: `LEDGER_SIGNER_*` ([`adapters/settings.py`](src/ledger_signer/adapters/settings.py)): the client
  roles' signer address and token, the signer's callers' tokens, backends, KMS and signing skew (never below
  300 s, the skew a channel assumes).
- Certificates (`domain/certificates.py`): an import is refused unless the certificate is an RSA (2048 bits or
  more) signing certificate of its key, valid now and for at least the signing skew. Not checked yet: that its
  subject names the company (the channel's requirements, v2-M3b), that it chains to an accepted certification
  authority of the country, and revocation (v2-M5). A certificate serves one mode or both; installing one takes
  its modes from the company's active certificates, each retired `replaced` once it serves none. Re-importing the
  same certificate (under another idempotency key) therefore retires the old row and adds an identical one, with
  the same fingerprint.
- Custody (`adapters/custody`) is the only code that touches key material; only the signer's app may import it
  (import-linter's `signer-custody` contracts). A key (PKCS#8) or an entity's credential is sealed with
  AES-256-GCM under a data key of its own, bound to its certificate, company and organization as associated
  data together with the kind of material (a key never opens as a credential, nor the reverse); the KMS wraps
  the data key under the same context and unwraps it for every signature (no cache). Backends: `local` (the
  key in custody) and `remote` (a certification entity signs the SHA-256 of `SignedInfo`, never the
  document). The local-file KMS (`kms_local.py`) is for `local` and `test` only; it creates its key file in
  `local` (owner-only mode on POSIX; on Windows the file is protected by its directory's ACL alone, so keep it
  in a directory only your user can read). Until path validation exists, a certificate its issuer signed
  with anything but a SHA-2 hash may not sign.
- State (`adapters/postgres`): `PgUnits` and `bind(uow)` give the use cases their unit (certificates, events,
  audit rows, jobs and import keys over the platform's `Idempotency`, route `grpc ImportCertificate`); the
  partial unique indexes' refusal is `ActiveCertificateTakenError` at the statement or at the commit.
- Assembly: `assembly.py` (`MODULE`, entry point `ledger.modules: signer`). The client roles (`api`, `worker`,
  `receiver`) bind the gRPC client ([`adapters/grpc/client.py`](src/ledger_signer/adapters/grpc/client.py)) with
  their own token, or, unconfigured, stand-ins answering `signer_unavailable` (`signer.not_configured`); no
  client role's readiness calls the signer. Messages map to DTOs in one place, `adapters/grpc/codec.py`.
- HTTP (`adapters/http`, mounted by `api` over the client or the stand-ins): `POST /v1/certificates` (the
  PKCS#12 bundle as the raw `application/x-pkcs12` body, at most 2 MiB, streamed to `ImportCertificate` as it
  arrives and never parsed by the api; its password in `Ledger-Certificate-Password`; `?modes=` repeatable),
  `POST /v1/certificates/remote` (an entity's credential), `GET /v1/certificates` (`include_retired`, one
  page), `GET` and `DELETE /v1/certificates/{crt_…}` (among the certificates of the request's mode; a delete
  retires for every mode). Every route asserts the caller's role in the `Ledger-Company` (identity's API) before
  calling the signer, which trusts the company and actor it is sent. `Idempotency-Key` passes through to the
  signer, which keeps it (one operation, *import*, for both routes; a retry needs the manage permission still).
  The signer's refusals keep their codes (`problems.py`): its `INVALID` family 422, conflicts 409,
  `certificate_not_found` 404, a bundle past 2 MiB 413, one too slow 408, the signer out of reach 503 with
  `Retry-After: 5`; our own client's stream defects and unmapped answers 500 `internal`.
- Service (`adapters/grpc/server.py`, put together by `adapters/serving.py`): in the `signer` role the
  assembly checks every caller's token (present, 32+ visible ASCII characters, none shared, a `_PREVIOUS` one
  only beside a current one), the local-file KMS (`LEDGER_SIGNER_KMS_KEY_FILE` named, `local`/`test` only) and
  the backends (`remote` needs a certification entity, none exists yet: `entity_not_configured`), then serves
  `SignerService` on `LEDGER_SIGNER_GRPC_PORT` behind the platform's guard (allowlists `CALLER_METHODS`: api
  imports, describes and retires; worker and receiver sign and describe), binds its facade as `SignerPort` and
  `SignerApi`, consumes `identity.company.deactivated` (`signer.company_deactivations`: every active
  certificate retired `company_deactivated` in the inbox row's unit), runs `signer.expiry_notice` jobs and its
  own purges, and is ready when the service serves (`signer:grpc`) and the KMS answers (`signer:kms`). An
  import streams its header first, then at most 2 MiB of bundle, and must end within the import deadline;
  each call acts as the actor its caller forwarded (its call id repeated in the metadata), and a signature is
  audited with the caller the guard admitted. CPU is bounded: imports `LEDGER_SIGNER_IMPORT_CONCURRENCY` at
  once, signatures `LEDGER_SIGNER_SIGNING_CONCURRENCY`, calls 64 at once, and a bundle naming more than
  1,000,000 key-derivation iterations is refused unopened. Production cannot run the signer yet: the only KMS
  is the local file, refused outside `local` and `test`; `LEDGER_SIGNER_GRPC_PORT=0` is for tests only.
