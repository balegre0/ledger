# ledger-signer

Signing certificates in custody, and every signature made with them, in a process of their own (spec §3.7,
ADR 0010; plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-v2-m2b-signer.md`).

- Owns schema `signer`, isolated in the signer's role (`[data] isolated_in = "signer"`): its roles belong to the
  `ledger_signer` login alone, whose tables (`certificates`, `custody`) no other app's login can read; the
  worker's fan-out moves its outbox and inbox. Id prefix `crt`; permissions `signer.certificates.read` and
  `signer.certificates.manage`.
- Contract: [`signer.proto`](../../contracts/proto/ledger/signer/v1/signer.proto) (`ledger.signer.v1.SignerService`),
  its code generated by `ledger-devtools sync` under `adapters/grpc/_generated/v1/` (never edited).
- Public surface: `SignerApi` ([`api.py`](src/ledger_signer/api.py)) for modules; fiscal-kit's `SignerPort` for
  channels; events `signer.certificate.installed`, `.retired` and `.expiring` (public `certificate.expiring`);
  HTTP under `/v1/certificates`, mounted by `api`.
- Settings: `LEDGER_SIGNER_*` ([`adapters/settings.py`](src/ledger_signer/adapters/settings.py)): the client
  roles' signer address and token, the signer's callers' tokens, backends, KMS and signing skew (never below
  300 s, the skew a channel assumes).
- Certificates (`domain/certificates.py`): an import is refused unless the certificate is an RSA (2048 bits or
  more) signing certificate of its key, valid now and for at least the signing skew. Not checked yet: that its
  subject names the company (the channel's requirements, v2-M3b), that it chains to an accepted certification
  authority of the country, and revocation (v2-M5). A certificate serves one mode or both; installing one takes
  its modes from the company's active certificates, each retired `replaced` once it serves none. Re-importing the
  same certificate (under another idempotency key) therefore retires the old row and adds an identical one, with
  the same fingerprint.
- Custody (`adapters/custody`) is the only code that touches key material; only the signer's app may import it
  (import-linter's `signer-custody` contracts). A key (PKCS#8) or an entity's credential is sealed with
  AES-256-GCM under a data key of its own, bound to its certificate, company and organization as associated
  data; the KMS wraps the data key under the same context and unwraps it for every signature (no cache).
  Backends: `local` (the key in custody) and `remote` (a certification entity signs the SHA-256 of
  `SignedInfo`, never the document). The local-file KMS (`kms_local.py`) is for `local` and `test` only; a
  certificate its issuer signed with SHA-1 or MD5 may not sign.
- State (`adapters/postgres`): `PgUnits` and `bind(uow)` give the use cases their unit (certificates, events,
  audit rows, jobs and import keys over the platform's `Idempotency`, route `grpc ImportCertificate`); the
  partial unique indexes' refusal is `ActiveCertificateTakenError` at the statement or at the commit.
- Assembly: `assembly.py` (`MODULE`, entry point `ledger.modules: signer`).
