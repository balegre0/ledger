## Across the stack

- **Never resend.** The DGII submission mark is the fence (`advancing.py`). `outcome_unknown` never returns to
  `signed`. A 429, a missing token, or a 401 after renewal means DGII certainly did not take it, so the mark is
  cleared and the document is tried later; a 5xx, a broken connection or a timeout after sending is an unknown
  outcome. The one physical resend is the 401 re-POST inside a single call, recorded in the plan and the runbooks.
  Commercial approvals follow the same rule (`approving.py`); buyer deliveries and the emitter side resend the
  identical archived bytes by design.
- **Tenancy.** Every fiscal and do_ecf tenant table is under forced row-level security; `receiver_seeds` and
  `receiver_tokens` sit outside it by design with their company filter pinned by a test. Receptions are recorded
  in the mode their environment implies. Open: `SignSeed` for the receiver and the receiver's login
  (least privilege, BACKLOG §1 item 2).
- **Secrets, tax ids, payloads.** Outbound spans carry method, host and status only; `url.path`, `url.query` and
  `url.full` are redacted; channel log events carry reasons and codes; `do_ecf.reception` logs company id,
  environment and outcome; error messages are fixed text. `invoicing.after_issue_failed` logs a traceback without
  locals: safe today, worth a look for new exception types.
- **The 2026-10-26 spec amendments** (Task 35's address, Task 26's api signing, §8.5's budget) agree with each
  other and the code, except the ADR bullet fixed here (M4).
